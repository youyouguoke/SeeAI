This page deliberately stops at four questions — role, access, action, and governance. They are the first questions an enterprise must answer before bringing in an AI work actor, and they define the boundary between human and AI responsibility. How AI actually completes complex tasks — goal decomposition, planning, tool use, and multi-step execution — belongs to the mechanism layer of the Agents concept, and is left to the next page.
