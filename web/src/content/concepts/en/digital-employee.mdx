A digital employee may be implemented with mechanisms such as agents — but "digital employee" describes its role and work boundaries in the enterprise, not a specific technical architecture.

Together with Ontology, it forms the first connection on SeeAI's main line: Ontology answers "what exists in the enterprise and how things are related," providing reason-able business context; the digital employee answers "who works here, what it can access, and what it can do," turning context into governed actions. Without context, it would not know which objects to act on; without boundaries, it would not belong in the enterprise.

Next, the Agents concept will open the mechanism layer of "how complex tasks get done" — goal decomposition, planning, tool use, and multi-step execution. This page deliberately stops at four questions: role, access, action, and governance. They are the first questions an enterprise must answer before bringing in an AI work actor — and they define the boundary between human and AI responsibility.
